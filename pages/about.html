About Inclusive Heritage

Enhancing immersion and accessibility at Mysore Palace through digital solutions and inclusive tourism.

Mysore Palace

Mysore Palace is one of India's most famous heritage monuments, known for its magnificent architecture, royal history and cultural significance. Inclusive Heritage aims to improve visitor experience by providing online ticket booking, digital payment facilities, multilingual support and easy access to important information.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make heritage tourism more accessible, convenient and engaging for every visitor using modern digital technology.
